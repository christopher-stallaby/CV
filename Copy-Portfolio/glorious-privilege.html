Worship: A Glorious Privilege

Psalm 19:1-2 "The heavens declare the glory of God, and the sky above proclaims His handiwork. Day to day pours out speech and night to night pours out knowledge." Romans 1:19-20 "For what can be known about God is plain to them, because god has shown it to them. For His invisible attributes, namely, His eternal power and divine nature, have been clearly perceived, ever since the creation of the world, in the things that have been made." Luke 19:40 " He (Jesus) answered, 'I tell you, if these [people] were silent, the very stones themselves would cry out.'"

All creation exists to worship God. Stars, clouds, trees, grass, dogs, cats, fish, turtles, ducks, bees, the entirety of the universe exists for God's glory, even angels: cherubim and seraphim and living creatures. All these were created for the purpose of giving worship and praise to God. On some level they do not really have a choice to do this because it is His intended purpose for them. Human beings however, are created with FREE WILL. We have choice, and God honors this gift of free will that He has given us by allowing us to make the choices we want, whether they are good for us or pleasing to Him or not. Of course, He wants us to make choices that are pleasing to Him, but He refuses to violate our free will. One of the choices we can make is to worship Him. When we do, it is not an obligation that we must perform. We are not doing our chores like slaves when we worship God. We are using our FREE WILL to make a choice and engage in an activity in which our involvement is a privilege. Worshipping God is a privilege for us, other creatures to whom God has not given free will the act of worship is at God's pleasure to employ from them. What an honor for us to join along with the rest of creation and lend our voice to the universal ensemble. When we choose to participate in the chorus of creation we have a unique ability to please God's heart simply because we have chosen to glorify Him. God desires love above all else.In our love relationship with our Creator, worship is both intimate and natural. FREE WILL is God's gift to human beings, He is pleased when we return this gift to Him for His glory and honor. Worship is probably the ultimate act a person can do which brings the Divine enjoyment to its climax. God delights in the praises of His people, so much so, that He said it is where He establishes His throne. What a privilege we have to please God, and join in the rest of creation by choice in worshipping our Creator and Savior. We especially move His heart because we are His unique creatures that have the choice to love and worship Him or hate and reject Him. Let's set aside special time this week to make sure that we are engaging our hearts with His in the ultimate love expression that is worship.
