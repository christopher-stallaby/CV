The Beauty of Jesus

Why is Jesus worth the price, why is He worth my life? I think this is an important question to ask ourselves because after all we only have one life to live, and if we give our lives to Jesus we better be certain that He deserves it.

A number of weeks ago our pastor spoke to us about counting the cost and considering Jesus to be worth the price He is asking us to pay. To be honest Jesus is asking a very high price, the highest in fact. So counting the cost and considering Jesus to be worth that cost is not something we can do lightly or in a moment. It's a constant choice, a relentless deciding that Jesus is worth it, but what I want to talk about this morning is why? Why is Jesus worth the price, why is He worth my life? I think this is an important question to ask ourselves because after all we only have one life to live, and if we give our lives to Jesus we better be certain that He deserves it. Also, God gave us free will, so I don't think that He is offended at us for asking difficult questions like this. So now I ask you, why is Jesus worth following? We may all have different answers, and that's ok. If I come back in 10 or 20 years and ask you again why Jesus is worth following, your answer then may be different than what it is now and again that's ok. As long as you know why Jesus is worth living and dying for, for you personally then you have something to hold onto when it looks like life isn't going according to plan. If you can't tell why Jesus is worth it, or maybe you don't believe that He is worth any of your life at all still, don't panic. Today I want to tell you guys why Jesus is worth it for me. Why I have counted the cost and considered Jesus to be well worth the rest of my life, my death should He ever ask for it. For me, Jesus is worth it because He is beautiful. When I talk about the beauty of Jesus or say that He is beautiful I don't mean physically like a beautiful woman, or a handsome man or a beautiful sunset. And in fact, the Bible says that He had no form or appearance that would make us desire Him. So outwardly Jesus looked like just your average Joe. But what is beautiful about Jesus is His character, who He really is at the core and center of His being. If we are honest, that is what really makes a person beautiful after all. I mean, you can have the best looks in the world, but if you are a cruel person then you're just ugly, good looks won't save a bad character. The first characteristic or attribute of Jesus that I want to talk about is love. The Bible says that God is love. Let's just think about that for a moment, th Bible is God's Word written down and given to all men. Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks, so the Bible is God's declaration of Himself, what He thinks how He feels and acts, He is describing Himself for everyone, everywhere for all time to have the opportunity to know. When it comes down to the wire, God gives a summary of Himself and He says I am love. Jesus said you've seen Me you have seen the Father (talking about God). Everything that God is Jesus is too, and the most important statement in my estimation is this one. God is love. If you know nothing else about Him, know this, that He is love. This love isn't just some strong feeling, or overwhelming emotion. No! Jesus' love is His identity. Love is His nature, He can not do or be anything but loving and so everything He does and says is loving. Because love is the nature of Jesus He chooses to always do what is best for others in spite of the consequences to Himself and whether He feels like it or not and regardless of whether we are worthy or not. Someone said it this way, everything that God does is for the greatest good or the largest number of people for the longest time. It's His choice to form us even in the womb, giving gifts and talents to us that will help us discover the plan that He has set in motion and how we can work with Him. It's His choice to heal our bodies, and to provide for our needs. It's His choice to sustain the earth on which we live so that we can have air to breathe and gravity to keep us attached to the ground. It's His choice to always be with us and never leave us no matter where we go or what we do. It was His choice to take our place on the cross while we still cursed Him and hated Him so that we could at least have the option of being in a relationship with Him should we ever change our minds. All this because He is love. Even in the full knowledge of the wrongs we have committed, the evil intentions we have had He still chooses to do what is best for you and me for no other reason than because He wants to. He loves us, so He wants to do what is best for us. Now I want to talk about a characteristic that is unique to Jesus. Of every other religion or cult or mindset that is out there I do not know of any one that has a "God" that possesses this attribute, that is humility. Any other god that people would choose to believe in has no problem ordering people about. Do this, do that, don't do this don't do that. But someone once said that the Christian God is the only God who has the courage to take His own medicine. This humility isn't just a means to an end, He wasn't acting this way to earn our respect or to prove a point. Again, humility, like love, is His nature. See, Jesus not only gave the law to men but He became a man Himself and followed His own law. He behaves as He would tell us to behave. The Bible says that Jesus when he became a man He took on the form of a servant. Since He was a servant He was obedient to the point of death, even the death of the cross. The cross is the most shameful death you could ever endure, and Jesus is completely innocent of all wrong-doing. Wrongfully accused, yet He did not say one word in His own defense. He stood there as He was being mocked and beaten, yet did not accuse those who were mistreating Him of any evil. Instead, He pardons them and asks His Father God to do the same. No other person ever has or ever will suffer like Jesus suffered and He endured all that without so much as a complaint. How humble do you have to be to accept a death that you know you do not deserve? I don't know if I could. That's why humility is one of the reasons Jesus is beautiful to me. Because He was not just humble, but He is more humble than any one else. The last characteristic I want to talk about this morning is His faithfulness. For me this is the fact that Jesus is always the same, He never changes. The Bible says that Jesus is the same yesterday, today and forever. In Him there is no changing or even a shadow of turning. Jesus has never been different than He is now, He never will be different than He has always been. He is dependable, trustworthy, tried and proven He has been tested under every circumstance and still He remains to be who He has always been, Loving and Humble. When we see His love and His humility through the lens of His faithfulness then we can realize that nothing we have done has earned His love and so nothing we can do to lose His love. He is convinced to love us, He has made up His mind, despite the fact that we are not worthy and even if we refuse to love Him back, He always has and always will love us as much now as He did on the cross. He is also always Humble, no matter the fame or the shame that people would put on Him. When He is exalted and lifted up He is humble, when His name is cursed He doesn't change. There is nothing in all existence that can change the humble nature of God because He is faithful.
